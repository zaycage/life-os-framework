# Principles

> Principles describe who we choose to become.

---

# What

Principles are values in written form.

They are not goals.

They are not metrics.

They are decisions about identity, made in advance.

---

# Why

In difficult moments, people fall to the level of what they have decided ahead of time.

Written principles remove the need to re-decide who you are every time life applies pressure.

They are the constitution beneath every system.

---

# The Principles

## Prepare Before the Storm

Life is unpredictable.

The responsibility is not to predict every storm, but to become the kind of person who can endure them.

Every investment in health, mind, finances, relationships, and character is preparation for challenges not yet visible.

Question

> If the storm arrived this year, would I be ready?

---

## Ownership

Take responsibility for decisions, actions, and outcomes.

Do not wait for life to happen.

Question

> Am I acting, or waiting?

---

## Discipline

Do what needs to be done, especially when motivation is absent.

Systems create discipline more reliably than willpower.

Question

> Did I show up today?

---

## Integrity

Keep your word.

Tell the truth.

Do the right thing when no one is watching.

Trust is earned slowly and lost quickly.

Question

> Would I be proud if this were visible to everyone?

---

## Courage

Choose action over avoidance.

Face difficult conversations.

Act despite uncertainty.

Question

> What am I avoiding right now?

---

## Patience

Meaningful things compound slowly.

Impatience abandons systems before they pay off.

Question

> Am I quitting, or is it just early?

---

## Curiosity

Keep learning.

Ask better questions.

Assume there is always another level of understanding.

Question

> When did I last change my mind?

---

## Humility

Stay teachable.

Strength without humility becomes brittle.

Question

> What might I be wrong about?

---

## Endurance

Some seasons are not for building.

Some seasons are for holding on.

Enduring a hard season without abandoning your principles is not failure.

It is the principles working.

Question

> Is this a season for optimizing, or a season for enduring?

---

# Applications

Principles are applied through:

- Decision Filters — principles turned into questions
- Systems — principles turned into repeatable behavior
- Reviews — principles used as the standard for reflection

A principle that never changes a decision is decoration.
