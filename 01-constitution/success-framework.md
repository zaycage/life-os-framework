# Success Framework

## Principles

How I choose to live.

See [Principles](./principles.md) for the full definitions.

## Levers

What I can intentionally improve.

- Systems
- Friction
- Energy
- Direction
- Surface area
- Preparedness

## Outcomes

What naturally emerges.

- Consistency
- Compounding
- Luck
- Success

## Success Equation

$$
Success =
\frac{(Systems \times Consistency \times Time)^{Direction} \times Luck}
{Friction}
$$

Where:

$$
Luck = 1 + (\text{Surface Area} \times \text{Preparedness})
$$

The equation describes the mechanics of success. The three-layer framework explains how a person operates those mechanics.
